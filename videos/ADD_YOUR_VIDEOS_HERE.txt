SANIYA ANAHITA LOVE PROPOSAL WEBSITE

1. Open index.html to preview locally (some browsers restrict local video autoplay).
2. Upload the whole folder to Netlify Drop to publish it.
3. To add your videos, copy the video files into this folder:
   videos/video1.mp4
   videos/video2.mp4
   videos/video3.mp4
   The website checks these files, plays them in order, then continues to the photos.
4. Photos are already included in assets/. The eye close-up is shown first.
5. Romantic instrumental-style sound is generated by the page after the visitor taps Yes.
   Browsers require a tap before sound can begin.
